Fluffy Pancakes

Soft, golden pancakes for a happy morning.

Ingredients

1 cup flour, 1 egg, ¾ cup milk, 1 tbsp sugar, 1 tsp baking powder, butter.

Steps

Mix ingredients into a smooth batter. Pour onto a warm greased pan. Cook until bubbles appear, flip and cook until golden.

🍝

Vegetable Pasta

A colorful and comforting pasta dish.

Ingredients

2 cups pasta, mixed vegetables, 2 tbsp tomato sauce, garlic, salt, pepper, oil.

Steps

Boil pasta and drain. Sauté garlic and vegetables in oil. Add sauce and seasoning, mix in pasta and serve warm.

🍰

Chocolate Cake

A rich chocolate treat for every occasion.

Ingredients

1 cup flour, ½ cup sugar, ¼ cup cocoa, 1 egg, ½ cup milk, ¼ cup oil, 1 tsp baking powder.

Steps

Mix dry ingredients, then add egg, milk and oil. Pour into a greased tin and bake at 180°C for about 25–30 minutes. Cool before serving.

🥪

Veggie Sandwich

A quick snack packed with fresh vegetables.

Ingredients

4 bread slices, cucumber, tomato, lettuce, cheese, butter, salt and pepper.

Steps

Spread butter on bread. Layer vegetables and cheese, season lightly, close the sandwich and toast until crisp.

🥤

Fruit Smoothie

A refreshing drink to start your day.

Ingredients

1 banana, ½ cup strawberries, 1 cup milk or yogurt, 1 tsp honey.

Steps

Add all ingredients to a blender. Blend until smooth. Pour into a glass and enjoy chilled.

🧁

Strawberry Cupcakes

Sweet little cupcakes with fruity flavor.

Ingredients

1 cup flour, ½ cup sugar, 1 egg, ½ cup milk, ¼ cup butter, chopped strawberries, baking powder.

Steps

Mix ingredients gently. Divide into cupcake liners and bake at 180°C for 18–20 minutes. Let cool before serving.
